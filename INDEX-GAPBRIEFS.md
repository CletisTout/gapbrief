## 💡 HELPFUL TIPS

### For Writers
- ✅ **Start with the FAQ section** - Use FAQ questions to guide your section writing
- ✅ **Follow H2 structure strictly** - It's designed for SEO & readability
- ✅ **Include competitor mentions** - Entity strategy tells you when to mention LG, Sony, etc.
- ✅ **Use comparison tables** - Gap #2 especially needs the comparison table as a key element
- ✅ **Write for your audience** - Gap #1 is consumer-friendly, Gap #3 is technical
- ✅ **Don't wait for perfection** - Submit by deadline, revisions happen after

### For Editors
- ✅ **Focus on accuracy first** - Specs, model numbers, competitor claims
- ✅ **Check Own Brand positioning** - Must be positive & modern (not defensive)
- ✅ **Verify competitor fairness** - LG, Sony, TCL mentioned honestly but clearly differentiated
- ✅ **Count sections** - Gap #1 needs 10 H2s, Gap #2 needs 12, Gap #3 needs 14
- ✅ **Check FAQ completeness** - 8-12 questions with detailed answers
- ✅ **Provide specific feedback** - Not just "rewrite this" but "change this to..."

### For Project Managers
- ✅ **Use DAILY-CHECKLIST daily** - Check off items, report status
- ✅ **Unblock writers quickly** - They shouldn't wait for answers
- ✅ **Flag issues early** - Don't wait until EOD to report problems
- ✅ **Celebrate milestones** - Team morale matters in 15-day sprint
- ✅ **Track word counts** - Count them at submission to catch issues early
- ✅ **Plan ahead** - Research materials should be ready before writers start
